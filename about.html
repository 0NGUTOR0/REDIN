About REDIN

About REDIN Group

REDIN Group is the umbrella organization for REDIN Initiative, REDIN Global Resources Ltd. and REDIN Industries. Together, these arms develop practical environmental solutions that promote responsible resource use, strengthen compliance and support sustainable economic development.

REDIN Group works across recycling, resource recovery, environmental consulting, research, policy engagement, education, capacity building, and clean industry development. REDIN Initiative leads nonprofit compliance and producer-responsibility work. REDIN Global Resources Ltd. delivers end-of-life vehicle and emission-testing programs. REDIN Industries develops and builds environmental industrial facilities.

Building on years of experience in recycling, waste management, battery stewardship, environmental advocacy, feasibility studies, and public-sector consulting, REDIN is expanding its capabilities in environmental compliance, producer responsibility operations, recycling industries, end-of-life vehicle management, vehicle-emission testing, and renewable energy.

REDIN also provides consulting, advisory, training, and environmental education services to individuals, companies, institutions, communities, and public-sector organizations seeking to improve environmental performance and adopt more sustainable practices in line with global best practices and technologies.

Our ambition is to become Nigeria's leading environmental and circular economy organization, recognized for practical solutions, environmental leadership, innovation, education, research, and sustainable impact, while building a strong regional and global presence.

Our Mission

Protecting the environment through practical action

To design and implement practical solutions through recycling, regulatory implementation support, consulting, research, education, capacity building, and strategic partnerships that protect the environment, strengthen compliance, recover value from resources, and support sustainable economic development.

Our Vision

Nigeria's leader in circular economy solutions

To be a leading sustainability, compliance, and circular economy organization in Nigeria, recognized regionally and globally for practical solutions, innovation, education, and measurable environmental impact.

Our Philosophy

Protect the Environment. Recover Value. Build Sustainable Systems.

At REDIN Group, we believe sustainability must move beyond awareness to practical action, responsible systems, and measurable outcomes. Our philosophy is built on environmental stewardship, circularity, responsible production and consumption, collaboration, and shared value.

We believe materials that have reached the end of one useful life should, wherever possible, be recovered, reused, recycled, or redirected into productive economic activity rather than becoming environmental liabilities.

For this reason, REDIN Group combines policy engagement, program implementation, recycling industries, consulting, education, research, partnerships and practical environmental solutions to help create systems that are environmentally responsible, economically viable and capable of delivering long-term public value.

  • Environmental stewardship

  • Integrity and accountability

  • Collaboration

  • Innovation and continuous learning

  • Shared environmental and economic value
